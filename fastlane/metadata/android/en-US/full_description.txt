Nomad Days counts your days abroad so you stay within visa-free limits and tax-residency thresholds.

<b>Estimates only, not legal or tax advice.</b> Rules change, so check official sources before you travel.

Log your trips (country, arrival and departure). The app shows, for each region you've visited:

* days used and days left
* the last day you can safely stay
* when you can come back

<b>Rules included</b>

* Schengen Area: 90 days in any 180
* United Kingdom: up to 6 months per visit, and the Statutory Residence Test for each tax year (6 April to 5 April)
* United States: 90 days per entry (Visa Waiver Program), and the substantial presence test

You can add your own rules for countries that aren't bundled yet: a limit per visit, a rolling window (for example 90 days in 180), or a tax-days threshold.

<b>Plan ahead</b>

Add future trips as Booked or Maybe and see straight away whether they fit:

* the first day a trip would break a rule, or how many days you'd have to spare
* how many tax days you'd have left in each country after the trip
* Booked trips count toward each other; a Maybe trip is checked on its own, so you can try ideas without affecting anything else
* a reminder a week before a booked trip that wouldn't fit, and "Did you go?" on the day it starts, so your log stays accurate

<b>UK sufficient ties</b>

Spending fewer than 183 days in the UK can still make you UK tax resident if you have enough UK ties. Answer a few yes/no questions (family, accommodation, work, and whether you were UK resident before), each with a plain-English explanation. The app also works out the 90-day and country ties from the trips you log and shows the number of UK days that would make you resident this tax year.

<b>Private by design</b>

* No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads
* Your data is encrypted on the device (SQLCipher, AES-256)
* Backups are encrypted files that you export and keep, protected by a passphrase only you know

Reminders are scheduled on the device and don't use Google services.
