Nomad Days counts your days abroad so you stay within visa-free limits and tax-residency thresholds.

<b>Estimates only, not legal or tax advice.</b> Rules change, so check official sources before you travel.

Log your trips (country, arrival and departure). The app shows, for each region you've visited:

* days used and days left
* the last day you can safely stay
* when you can come back

<b>Rules included</b>

* Schengen Area: 90 days in any 180
* United Kingdom: up to 6 months per visit, and 183 days in the tax year (Statutory Residence Test)
* United States: 90 days per entry (Visa Waiver Program), and the substantial presence test

You can add your own rules for countries that aren't bundled yet: a limit per visit, a rolling window (for example 90 days in 180), or a tax-days threshold.

<b>Private by design</b>

* No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads
* Your data is encrypted on the device (SQLCipher, AES-256)
* Backups are encrypted files that you export and keep, protected by a passphrase only you know

Reminders are scheduled on the device and don't use Google services.
